## 1. Mathematical Architecture & Blueprint Deconstruction (`gromov_compactness.pdf`)

### 1.1 Target Manifold, Almost Complex Structures, and Symplectic Energy
Following `gromov_compactness.pdf` §2.1 and Gromov (1985):
- Let $M$ be a compact, boundaryless, finite-dimensional smooth manifold.
- Let $\omega$ be a smooth closed non-degenerate two-form on $M$.
- Let $J \in \Gamma(\operatorname{End}(TM))$ be a smooth almost complex structure ($J^2 = -\operatorname{id}_{TM}$) that is **tamed by $\omega$**:
  $$\omega(v, Jv) > 0 \quad \forall x \in M, \; \forall v \in T_x M \setminus \{0\}.$$
- **Symmetrized Riemannian Metric**: 
  $$g(v, w) := \frac{1}{2}\big(\omega(v, Jw) + \omega(w, Jv)\big).$$
  This is a positive-definite, $J$-invariant Riemannian metric on $M$. For compatible $J$, $\omega(v, Jw)$ is already symmetric and equals $g(v, w)$. Under mere tameness, $\omega(v, Jw)$ is not assumed symmetric.
- **Pseudo-holomorphic Curve**: A smooth map $u: \Sigma \to M$ from a Riemann surface $(\Sigma, j)$ satisfying:
  $$du \circ j = J \circ du \iff \bar\partial_J u = \frac{1}{2}(du + J \circ du \circ j) = 0.$$
- **Symplectic Energy Measure**: For any measurable subset $A \subseteq \Sigma$,
  $$E(u; A) := \frac{1}{2} \int_A |du|_g^2 \, d\mathrm{vol}_\Sigma, \quad E(u) := E(u; \Sigma).$$
  For any $J$-holomorphic curve, $E(u; A) = \int_A u^* \omega$. In particular, the energy measure is non-negative, conformally invariant, and additive. Points and smooth boundary circles have measure zero.

### 1.2 Genus 0 Domain: The Riemann Sphere $\mathbb{C}P^1$
The domain is $\mathbb{C}P^1 = \mathbb{C} \cup \{\infty\}$ with its standard two-chart complex structure ($z$ and $1/z$). 
- Bi-holomorphic automorphisms are precisely Möbius transformations $\operatorname{PSL}(2, \mathbb{C})$, generated by affine maps $z \mapsto a + \lambda z$ ($\lambda > 0$, $a \in \mathbb{C}$), rotations, and inversion $z \mapsto 1/z$.
- In disk coordinates $D_r(a) = \{z : |z - a| < r\}$ and annuli $A(r, R; a) = D_R(a) \setminus D_r(a)$, open and closed conventions do not alter energy values.

### 1.3 Labelled Sphere Trees and Stability
As defined in `gromov_compactness.pdf` §2.2:
- A **labelled sphere tree** consists of:
  1. A non-empty finite tree $T = (V, E_T)$ (connected, acyclic graph, $b_1(T) = 0$).
  2. Component curves $u_v: \mathbb{C}P^1 \to M$ for each $v \in V$.
  3. A nodal attachment point $z_{vw} \in \mathbb{C}P^1$ on component $v$ for each oriented edge $v \to w \in E_T$.
  4. Pairwise distinctness: for each $v$, the points $\{z_{vw} : w \sim v\}$ are pairwise distinct.
  5. Nodal matching: for each edge $v \sim w$,
     $$u_v(z_{vw}) = u_w(z_{wv}).$$
- **Stability Condition**: Every component $v \in V$ on which $u_v$ is **constant** (a "ghost" component) must have at least three incident edges: $\operatorname{deg}(v) \ge 3$. (Non-constant components require no lower bound on their degree).
- For $k$-marked trees, $k$ marked points are attached to distinct smooth points disjoint from nodes, counting toward stability ($2g_v - 2 + n_v > 0$).

### 1.4 The Compactness Witness and Gromov Convergence Topology
As specified in `gromov_compactness.pdf` §2.3:
For a sequence of non-constant $J$-holomorphic spheres $u_n: \mathbb{C}P^1 \to M$, a **compactness witness** consists of a strictly increasing subsequence $\sigma: \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$, a sphere tree $(T, \{u_v\}, \{z_{vw}\})$, and Möbius coordinate frames $\phi_{v, n}: \mathbb{C}P^1 \to \mathbb{C}P^1$ satisfying:
1. **$C^\infty_{\mathrm{loc}}$ Component Convergence**:
   $$u_{\sigma(n)} \circ \phi_{v, n} \to u_v \quad \text{in } C^\infty \text{ on compact subsets of } \mathbb{C}P^1 \setminus \{z_{vw} : w \sim v\}.$$
2. **Neighboring Frame Collapse**: For every edge $v \sim w$,
   $$\phi_{v, n}^{-1} \circ \phi_{w, n} \to z_{vw} \quad \text{locally uniformly on } \mathbb{C}P^1 \setminus \{z_{wv}\}.$$
3. **Total Energy Conservation**:
   $$\lim_{n \to \infty} E(u_{\sigma(n)}) = \sum_{v \in V} E(u_v).$$
4. **Vanishing Neck Image Diameter**: In annular coordinates, every neck connecting components has vanishing image diameter:
   $$\lim_{R \to \infty} \lim_{\delta \to 0} \lim_{n \to \infty} \operatorname{diam} u_n(A(R\lambda_n, \delta; a_n)) = 0.$$

### 1.5 The Master Compactness Theorem (Theorem 1 of `gromov_compactness.pdf`)
> **Theorem 1 (Core Target)**: Let $u_n: \mathbb{C}P^1 \to M$ be non-constant $J$-holomorphic maps and let $B \ge 0$ satisfy $E(u_n) \le B$ for every $n$. 
> 1. There is a subsequence with a stable sphere tree limit in the compactness witness sense above.
> 2. There is a target-dependent constant $h > 0$ such that the number $P$ of non-constant components satisfies:
>    $$P \cdot h \le B.$$
> 3. If $|V| > 1$ and $G$ components are constant ("ghosts"), then:
>    $$G \le P - 2, \quad \text{hence } |V| = P + G \le 2P - 2.$$
>    (A one-vertex non-constant tree $|V|=1, P=1, G=0$ is also allowed).

## 2. Hofer's Lemma and the Local Rescaling Engine

### 2.1 The Mathlib Engine: `Mathlib.Analysis.Hofer`
In Mathlib, Patrick Massot's theorem `hofer` provides the metric space extraction tool:
```lean
theorem hofer {X : Type*} [MetricSpace X] [CompleteSpace X] (x : X) (ε : ℝ) (ε_pos : 0 < ε)
    {ϕ : X → ℝ} (cont : Continuous ϕ) (nonneg : ∀ y, 0 ≤ ϕ y) : ∃ ε' > 0, ∃ x' : X,
    ε' ≤ ε ∧ d x' x ≤ 2 * ε ∧ ε * ϕ x ≤ ε' * ϕ x' ∧ ∀ y, d x' y ≤ ε' → ϕ y ≤ 2 * ϕ x'
```

### 2.2 Integration with `gromov_compactness.pdf` Rescaling
In `gromov_compactness.pdf` §3.3 & §5 (Tasks B01–B03):
- Rather than an arbitrary blow-up sequence, the blueprint establishes a **calibrated rescaling**:
  1. Fix universal energy threshold $h > 0$ satisfying:
     $$0 < h \le \min(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{reg}}/2, \, \varepsilon_{\mathrm{sph}}, \, \varepsilon_{\mathrm{neck}}/2).$$
  2. For a concentration point $0 \in D_1$ with defect mass $m \ge \varepsilon_{\mathrm{reg}} \ge h$, select centers $a_n \in D_{1/2}$ maximizing the Euclidean gradient $|df_n|$.
  3. By continuity of the radial energy, select radii $\lambda_n > 0$ satisfying the **exact calibration equation**:
     $$E(f_n; D_{\lambda_n}(a_n)) = m - \frac{h}{2}.$$
  4. The rescaled sequence $v_n(z) = f_n(a_n + \lambda_n z)$ has gradient controlled on $D_1$ because $a_n$ maximized the gradient, and the domain expands to exhaust $\mathbb{C}$.

## 3. The Hard Proof Steps Exposed in `gromov_compactness.pdf`

### 3.1 Epsilon Regularity (Task A03)
There exist constants $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{reg}}, C > 0$ such that for any curve on $D_r$,
$$E(u; D_r) < \varepsilon_{\mathrm{reg}} \implies \sup_{D_{r/2}} |du|^2 \le C r^{-2} E(u; D_r).$$
- Proven via a Bochner differential inequality for the Euclidean energy density $e = \frac{1}{2}|du|^2$: $\Delta e \ge -b e^2$ (Task A02), followed by a scalar mean-value inequality (Task A01).
- The flat coordinate metric on $D_r$ means no domain-curvature terms appear.

### 3.2 Non-constant Sphere Energy Gap $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{sph}} > 0$ (Task A07)
Cover $S^2$ with fixed coordinate disks. If $E(u) < \varepsilon_{\mathrm{sph}}$, epsilon regularity bounds the global gradient, implying $\operatorname{diam} u(S^2) \le C \sqrt{E(u)}$. For sufficiently small energy, the image lies inside a single contractible chart where $\omega = d\alpha$. By Stokes' theorem, $E(u) = \int_{S^2} u^* \omega = \int_{S^2} d(u^* \alpha) = 0$, forcing $u$ to be constant! This proves $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{sph}} > 0$ without assuming rationality of $[\omega]$ or discreteness of symplectic periods.

### 3.3 Long Cylinder Decay and Neck Control (Tasks A10, B03)
On a conformal cylinder $[-L, L] \times S^1$, if $E(v) < \varepsilon_{\mathrm{neck}}$ and $2 \le T < L$:
$$E(v; [-L+T, L-T] \times S^1) \le C_0 e^{-aT} E(v),$$
$$\operatorname{diam} v([-L+T, L-T] \times S^1) \le C_0 e^{-aT/2} \sqrt{E(v)}.$$
This differential inequality integrates the short-loop action/isoperimetric estimate, proving that **no energy or distance survives in the neck** between bubbles.

### 3.4 Ghost Branching: Constant Rescaled Components Branch (Task B05)
A critical mathematical hurdle in stable curve theory: what if a bubble $v: \mathbb{C}P^1 \to M$ is constant?
- In `gromov_compactness.pdf` §3.5, the blueprint provides a complete resolution:
  - If $v$ is constant, since defect mass $m > 0$, there must be concentration points on $\mathbb{C}$.
  - The maximizing choice of $a_n$ forces $|dv_n(0)| \ge |dv_n(z)|$, creating a concentration atom at $z = 0$.
  - By calibration, the mass at $0$ is at most $m - h/2$.
  - Because total defect mass is $m$, the remaining mass must be distributed among at least one other finite concentration point!
  - Therefore, there are at least **two** finite concentration points $q_1, q_2 \in \mathbb{C}$.
  - Including the attachment point to the parent at $\infty$, the ghost sphere has at least **three** special points ($0, q_2, \infty$).
  - Hence, every constant component is **automatically stable** ($\operatorname{deg}(v) \ge 3$)!

### 3.5 Well-Founded Induction Replaces "Repeat Bubbling" (Task B06, B07)
Rather than an informal iterative "repeat until no more bubbles" argument, `gromov_compactness.pdf` §3.6 introduces a rigorous **well-founded induction**:
- Define the **energy rank** as the natural number:
  $$\operatorname{rank}(m) := \left\lfloor \frac{m}{h} \right\rfloor \in \mathbb{N}.$$
- If the bubble $v$ is non-constant, $E(v) \ge h$, so each child defect $m_i$ satisfies $m_i \le m - h$.
- If $v$ is constant, $v$ has at least two children, each contributing at least $h$, so again $m_i \le m - h$.
- In either case:
  $$\left\lfloor \frac{m_i}{h} \right\rfloor \le \left\lfloor \frac{m - h}{h} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{m}{h} \right\rfloor - 1 < \left\lfloor \frac{m}{h} \right\rfloor.$$
- The induction on $\mathbb{N}$ terminates in at most $\lfloor B / h \rfloor$ steps!

### 3.6 Global Assembly, Stabilization, and Component Bounds (Tasks G01–G04)
- Telescoping local affine frames yields global Möbius transformations on the original sphere.
- **Stabilization**: A constant root of degree 1 is removed; a constant root of degree 2 is suppressed (its two neighbors joined).
- **Component Count Identity**:
  For $|V| > 1$, every vertex has $\operatorname{deg}(v) \ge 1$ and each ghost has $\operatorname{deg}(v) \ge 3$.
  In any tree, the sum of degrees is $2(|V| - 1) = 2(P + G - 1)$.
  Thus:
  $$3G + 1 \cdot P \le \sum_{v \in V} \operatorname{deg}(v) = 2(P + G - 1) \implies G \le P - 2.$$
  Since $P \cdot h \le B$, we obtain the strict numerical bound:
  $$|V| = P + G \le 2P - 2 \le 2 \left\lfloor \frac{B}{h} \right\rfloor - 2.$$

  ω_nondegenerate : ∀ (x : M) (v : TangentSpace I x), (∀ w, ω x v w = 0) → v = 0
  /-- The almost complex structure endomorphism $J$. -/
  J : (x : M) → TangentSpace I x →L[ℝ] TangentSpace I x
  /-- $J^2 = -\operatorname{id}$. -/
  J_sq : ∀ (x : M) (v : TangentSpace I x), J x (J x v) = -v
  /-- Smoothness of $J$. -/
  J_smooth : ∀ (x : M), ContMDiffAt I 𝓘(ℝ, TangentSpace I x →L[ℝ] TangentSpace I x) ∞
    (fun y => J y) x
  /-- Symplectic tameness: $\omega(v, Jv) > 0$ for all nonzero $v$. -/
  tame : ∀ (x : M) (v : TangentSpace I x), v ≠ 0 → 0 < ω x v (J x v)

/-- Symmetrized Riemannian metric $g(v, w) = \frac{1}{2}(\omega(v, Jw) + \omega(w, Jv))$. -/
def ClosedTameTarget.metric [tgt : ClosedTameTarget I M] (x : M) (v w : TangentSpace I x) : ℝ :=
  (1 / 2) * (tgt.ω x v (tgt.J x w) + tgt.ω x w (tgt.J x v))

/-- Standard Riemann sphere $\mathbb{C}P^1$. -/
abbrev CP1 := Metric.sphere (0 : ℝ × ℝ × ℝ) 1
def I_CP1 : ModelWithCorners ℝ (ℝ × ℝ) (EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 2)) := sorry
def CP1.almostComplexStructure : (z : CP1) → TangentSpace I_CP1 z →L[ℝ] TangentSpace I_CP1 z := sorry

/-- A smooth map $u: \mathbb{C}P^1 \to M$ is $J$-holomorphic if $du \circ j = J \circ du$. -/
def JHolomorphicOn [tgt : ClosedTameTarget I M] (u : CP1 → M) (s : Set CP1) : Prop :=
  ∀ (z : CP1), z ∈ s → ∀ (v : TangentSpace I_CP1 z),
    mfderiv I_CP1 I u z (CP1.almostComplexStructure z v) =
      tgt.J (u z) (mfderiv I_CP1 I u z v)

/-- Symplectic energy measure $E(u; A) = \int_A u^* \omega$. -/
def energyMeasure [tgt : ClosedTameTarget I M] (u : CP1 → M) (A : Set CP1) : ℝ := sorry

/-- Total energy $E(u) = E(u; \mathbb{C}P^1)$. -/
def totalEnergy [tgt : ClosedTameTarget I M] (u : CP1 → M) : ℝ :=
  energyMeasure I M u univ

/-- Compactness witness for a sequence of spheres converging to a stable tree limit. -/
structure CompactnessWitness [tgt : ClosedTameTarget I M]

  frames : (v : u_lim.tree.V) → ℕ → (CP1 ≃ₜ CP1) /- Möbius maps -/

  neck_vanishing : True /- Quantified vanishing neck diameter condition -/

The number of non-constant components $P$ satisfies $P \cdot h \le B$, and the number of

    (th : TargetThresholds I M)
    (u_seq : ℕ → (CP1 → M)) (B : ℝ)
    (h_nonconst : ∀ n, ¬∃ c, ∀ z, u_seq n z = c)
    (h_jhol : ∀ n, JHolomorphicOn I M (u_seq n) univ)
    (h_energy : ∀ n, totalEnergy I M (u_seq n) ≤ B) :
    ∃ (u_lim : StableSphereTreeLimit I M),
      Nonempty (CompactnessWitness I M u_seq u_lim) ∧

      let G := {v : u_lim.tree.V // ∃ c, ∀ z, u_lim.map v z = c}.toFinset.card
      (P * th.h ≤ B) ∧
      (1 < Fintype.card u_lim.tree.V → G + 2 ≤ P ∧ Fintype.card u_lim.tree.V + 2 ≤ 2 * P) := sorry

end GromovCompactness

## 6. The 8 Mathematical Stress Cases (from `gromov_compactness.pdf` §6)

Before finalizing any blueprint or code, the design must be verified against the 8 stress cases identified in `gromov_compactness.pdf`:

1. **Non-compact Target $M = \mathbb{C}$**:
   - *Phenomenon*: Constant maps $u_n(z) = n$ have energy 0, but escape to infinity with no convergent subsequence.
   - *Design Solution*: Compactness of $M$ (`CompactSpace M`) is strictly required in the core theorem. For non-compact targets, all images must lie in a fixed compact set.
2. **Domain Collapse $u_n(z) = n z$ on $\mathbb{C}P^1 \to \mathbb{C}P^1$**:
   - *Phenomenon*: On the original domain, $u_n \to \infty$ locally uniformly on $\mathbb{C}^*$. Convergence on the original domain alone completely misses the non-constant bubble!
   - *Design Solution*: The compactness witness explicitly includes moving Möbius coordinate frames $\phi_{v, n}$.
3. **Constant Base with Multiple Concentration Clusters (Ghosts)**:
   - *Phenomenon*: If several bubbling points form on a component whose limiting map is constant, that component becomes a ghost. Ghosts carry 0 energy and cannot be detected or counted by energy bounds alone.
   - *Design Solution*: Task B05 directly proves that every rescaled ghost has $\ge 2$ children plus its parent node, ensuring $\ge 3$ incident edges (topological stability).
4. **Multiple Covers**:
   - *Phenomenon*: Sequences of multiple-cover spheres $z \mapsto z^d$ are valid $J$-holomorphic maps.
   - *Design Solution*: No step in the proof assumes injectivity, simplicity, or somewhere-injectivity.
5. **Concentration Points on the Calibration Boundary**:
   - *Phenomenon*: An atom could potentially land on the boundary circle of the calibration disk.
   - *Design Solution*: The margin $h/2$ in $E(f_n; D_{\lambda_n}(a_n)) = m - h/2$ prevents boundary atoms from disrupting the ghost-branching proof.
6. **Strict vs. Non-Strict Inequalities at Energy Thresholds**:
   - *Phenomenon*: Inconsistent bounds on energy gaps can cause circularity or off-by-one errors.
   - *Design Solution*: Fixed threshold record $0 < h \le \min(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{reg}}/2, \varepsilon_{\mathrm{sph}}, \varepsilon_{\mathrm{neck}}/2)$ (Task B01).
7. **Colliding Marked Points on Constant Spheres**:
   - *Phenomenon*: Three marked points colliding on a sphere with no gradient blow-up still forces domain reparametrization to preserve marked stability.
   - *Design Solution*: Extension task E03 explicitly handles marked configuration limits by ratio-of-differences normalization.
8. **Varying Homology Classes**:
   - *Phenomenon*: In general symplectic manifolds, maps $u_n$ can represent different homology classes with the same energy.
   - *Design Solution*: Fixed homology class is not assumed for extraction; extension task E01 proves preservation of integral homology $[u_{\sigma(n)}] = \sum [u_v]$ in $H_2(M; \mathbb{Z})$ via neck diameter bounds and Stokes' theorem.
