## 1. Executive Summary & Foundational Scope

### 1.1 The Objective
The goal of this project is the complete, kernel-verified formalization of **Gromov Compactness for Genus 0 Pseudo-Holomorphic Curves** ($J$-holomorphic spheres) in **Lean 4**. 

Given a compact, boundaryless symplectic manifold $(M, \omega)$ equipped with an $\omega$-tamed smooth almost complex structure $J$, and a sequence of non-constant $J$-holomorphic spheres $u_n: \mathbb{CP}^1 \to M$ with uniformly bounded symplectic energy:
$$E(u_n) = \int_{\mathbb{CP}^1} u_n^* \omega \le B < \infty,$$
the theorem establishes that a subsequence $u_{\sigma(n)}$ converges in the **Gromov topology** to a **stable nodal sphere tree** $(T, \{u_v\}_{v \in V}, \{z_{vw}\}_{v \sim w})$.

### 1.2 Quantitative Target Statement (Theorem 1)
Following `gromov_compactness.pdf` §2.3:
Let $u_n: \mathbb{CP}^1 \to M$ be non-constant $J$-holomorphic maps satisfying $E(u_n) \le B$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then:
1. **Subsequence & Stable Limit**: There exists a strictly increasing subsequence $\sigma: \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ and a stable sphere tree limit $(T, \{u_v\}, \{z_{vw}\})$ with coordinate Möbius frames $\phi_{v, n}: \mathbb{CP}^1 \to \mathbb{CP}^1$ realizing the compactness witness.
2. **Energy Conservation**: Total energy is conserved:
   $$\lim_{n \to \infty} E(u_{\sigma(n)}) = \sum_{v \in V} E(u_v).$$
3. **Vanishing Necks**: Every annular neck connecting bubble components has vanishing energy and image diameter in target distance:
   $$\lim_{R \to \infty} \lim_{\delta \to 0} \lim_{n \to \infty} \operatorname{diam} u_{\sigma(n)}(A(R\lambda_n, \delta; a_n)) = 0.$$
4. **Universal Energy Quantum**: There exists a target-dependent constant $h > 0$ such that the number $P$ of non-constant components satisfies:
   $$P \cdot h \le B.$$
5. **Ghost & Vertex Bounds**: If $|V| > 1$ and $G$ components are constant ("ghost bubbles"), then $G \le P - 2$ (unmarked) and $G \le P + k - 2$ ($k$-marked). Across all stable configurations (including the unbubbled case $|V| = 1$), the total vertex count satisfies:
   $$|V| \le \max\left(1, 2 \left\lfloor \frac{B}{h} \right\rfloor + k - 2\right).$$
   (For unmarked curves $k = 0$, when $|V| > 1$ we have $|V| \le 2P - 2 \le 2 \lfloor B/h \rfloor - 2$, and when $|V| = 1$ we have $|V| = 1 = \max(1, 0)$).

### 1.3 Scope and Deliberate Exclusions
- **Genus 0 Domain**: The domain is exclusively the Riemann sphere $\mathbb{CP}^1 = \mathbb{C} \cup \{\infty\}$. Bi-holomorphic automorphisms are $\operatorname{PSL}(2, \mathbb{C})$. There is no domain degeneration (Deligne-Mumford curve degeneration of the underlying abstract surface), only bubble tree formation generated by gradient blow-up and coordinate rescaling.
- **No Extraneous Hypotheses**: No semipositivity, rationality of $[\omega]$, genericity of $J$, simplicity, or somewhere-injectivity is assumed.
- **Gauge-Theoretic Separation**: Unlike Lipyanskiy's coupled Gromov-Uhlenbeck theorem, this formalization extracts the pure symplectic geometric curve theorem without ASD connection coupling.

### 1.4 The 'Sorry'-First-Pass Autoformalization Strategy
To prevent pipeline stalls and maximize engineering parallelization across the 39 contracts, the project adopts an explicit **'sorry'-first-pass strategy**:
1. **Unblocking Downstream Progress**: As a first pass, if a needed prerequisite result or auxiliary lemma is not readily available in Mathlib or TauCeti, its formal declaration and candidate type signature are established precisely, and the proof is left with `sorry`.
2. **Horizontal Interface Stability**: Downstream theorems (such as concentration compactness `C02`, bubbling calibration `B02`, global tree assembly `G01`, and the master theorem `G03`) proceed immediately using the exact type signatures and available upstream results without waiting for complex low-level analytic lemmas to be fully resolved.
3. **Iterative Infilling**: Proof stubs are systematically prioritized and tackled in subsequent passes using local Gemma/Ollama tactic search and Mathlib library alignment, culminating in complete `#print axioms` closure at Gate `G05`.

## 3. Concrete Mathematical Definitions

### 3.1 Target Geometry & Symmetrized Metric (`F01`)
Let $M$ be a compact, finite-dimensional, boundaryless smooth manifold of dimension $2n$.
- $\omega \in \Omega^2(M)$ is a smooth closed 2-form ($d\omega = 0$) such that for every $x \in M$, $\omega_x$ is non-degenerate.
- $J \in \Gamma(\operatorname{End}(TM))$ is a smooth almost complex structure: $J^2 = -\operatorname{id}_{TM}$.
- **Tameness**: $J$ is tamed by $\omega$ if for every $x \in M$ and non-zero $v \in T_x M$:
  $$\omega_x(v, J_x v) > 0.$$
- **Symmetrized Riemannian Metric**:
  $$g_x(v, w) := \frac{1}{2} \big( \omega_x(v, J_x w) + \omega_x(w, J_x v) \big).$$
  $g$ is positive definite, symmetric, and $J$-invariant: $g(Jv, Jw) = g(v, w)$. Under tameness alone, $\omega(v, Jw)$ is not assumed symmetric; symmetrization is mandatory.
- **Riemannian Distance**: On each connected component of $M$, $g$ induces the geodesic metric $d_g$. Since $M$ is compact, it has finitely many connected components. A connected curve $u(\mathbb{CP}^1)$ lies in a single connected component.

### 3.2 The Cauchy-Riemann Equation (`F01`, `F02`)
Let $(\Sigma, j)$ be a smooth Riemann surface. A smooth map $u: \Sigma \to M$ is **pseudo-holomorphic** (or $J$-holomorphic) if:
$$du \circ j = J \circ du \iff \bar{\partial}_{J, j} u := \frac{1}{2}(du + J \circ du \circ j) = 0.$$
In local complex coordinates $z = s + it$ on $\Sigma$, where $j(\partial_s) = \partial_t$ and $j(\partial_t) = -\partial_s$, this is equivalent to:
$$\partial_s u + J(u) \, \partial_t u = 0 \iff u_t = J(u) u_s.$$

### 3.3 Symplectic Energy Measure (`F03`)
For any measurable subset $A \subseteq \Sigma$, the energy is defined by:
$$E(u; A) := \frac{1}{2} \int_A |du|_g^2 \, d\mathrm{vol}_\Sigma.$$
For a $J$-holomorphic curve $u$, on every tangent 2-plane spanned by $(v, jv)$, we have:
$$|du(v)|_g^2 + |du(jv)|_g^2 = g(du(v), du(v)) + g(J du(v), J du(v)) = 2 \omega(du(v), du(jv)),$$
which precisely implies:
$$\frac{1}{2} |du|_g^2 \, d\mathrm{vol}_\Sigma = u^* \omega.$$
Hence, for $J$-holomorphic curves:
$$E(u; A) = \int_A u^* \omega.$$
**Properties**:
1. **Positivity**: $E(u; A) \ge 0$, with $E(u; \Sigma) = 0 \iff u$ is constant (on connected $\Sigma$).
2. **Conformal Invariance**: If $\phi: \Sigma' \to \Sigma$ is a bi-holomorphic map, then $E(u \circ \phi; \phi^{-1}(A)) = E(u; A)$.
3. **Zero Mass of Boundaries and Points**: Points and smooth circles have measure zero: $E(u; \{z_0\}) = 0$ and $E(u; \partial D_r) = 0$.

### 3.4 Labelled Sphere Trees & Stability (`F06`)
A **labelled sphere tree** representing a nodal genus-0 curve consists of:
1. A finite connected tree $T = (V, E_T)$ (acyclic graph, $b_1(T) = 0$).
2. For each vertex $v \in V$, a smooth $J$-holomorphic component map $u_v: \mathbb{CP}^1 \to M$.
3. For each directed edge $(v, w)$ with $\{v, w\} \in E_T$, a nodal attachment point $z_{vw} \in \mathbb{CP}^1$.
4. **Nodal Distinctness**: For each $v \in V$, the nodal points $\{z_{vw} : w \sim v\}$ are pairwise distinct in $\mathbb{CP}^1$.
5. **Nodal Matching**: For each edge $\{v, w\} \in E_T$:
   $$u_v(z_{vw}) = u_w(z_{wv}).$$
6. **Stability**: A component $v \in V$ is called a **ghost** if $u_v$ is constant. The tree is **stable** if every ghost component has at least three nodal points:
   $$\operatorname{deg}(v) \ge 3 \quad \text{for all } v \in V \text{ such that } u_v \text{ is constant}.$$
   (Non-constant components require no lower bound on degree).

### 3.5 The Compactness Witness (`F06`, `G03`)
A sequence $u_n: \mathbb{CP}^1 \to M$ converges to a stable sphere tree $(T, \{u_v\}, \{z_{vw}\})$ if there exist:
- A strictly increasing subsequence $\sigma: \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$,
- Möbius transformations $\phi_{v, n} \in \operatorname{PSL}(2, \mathbb{C})$ for each $v \in V$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$,
such that:
1. **$C^\infty_{\mathrm{loc}}$ Convergence**: For each vertex $v \in V$,
   $$u_{\sigma(n)} \circ \phi_{v, n} \longrightarrow u_v \quad \text{in } C^\infty(K, M)$$
   for every compact subset $K \subset \mathbb{CP}^1 \setminus \{z_{vw} : w \sim v\}$.
2. **Frame Transition Collapse**: For every edge $\{v, w\} \in E_T$,
   $$\phi_{v, n}^{-1} \circ \phi_{w, n} \longrightarrow z_{vw} \quad \text{locally uniformly on } \mathbb{CP}^1 \setminus \{z_{wv}\}.$$
3. **Global Energy Conservation**:
   $$\lim_{n \to \infty} E(u_{\sigma(n)}) = \sum_{v \in V} E(u_v).$$
4. **Vanishing Neck Image Diameter**: In the calibrated annular coordinates $A(R\lambda_n, \delta; a_n)$,
   $$\forall \eta > 0, \; \exists R_0, \; \forall R \ge R_0, \; \exists \delta_0, \; \forall \delta \in (0, \delta_0), \; \exists N, \; \forall n \ge N: \quad \operatorname{diam} u_n(A(R\lambda_n, \delta; a_n)) < \eta.$$

## 4. The Hard Proof Steps Exposed

Here we outline the eleven hard mathematical mechanisms specified in `gromov_compactness.pdf`:

### 4.1 Uniform Local Analysis & Epsilon Regularity (Tasks `A01`, `A02`, `A03`)
The foundation of bubbling analysis is the $\varepsilon$-regularity theorem:
$$\exists \varepsilon_{\mathrm{reg}} > 0, C > 0 \quad \text{such that} \quad E(u; D_r) < \varepsilon_{\mathrm{reg}} \implies \sup_{D_{r/2}} |du|^2 \le C r^{-2} E(u; D_r).$$
- **Proof Structure**:
  1. Derive the Bochner differential inequality for the energy density $e = \frac{1}{2}|du|^2$. In flat domain disk coordinates, the Laplace-Beltrami operator satisfies:
     $$\Delta e \ge -b e^2,$$
     where $b \ge 0$ depends only on the $C^2$ bounds of $J$ and the metric $g$ on the compact target $M$. Domain curvature vanishes identically because the disk metric is flat!
  2. Establish the scalar mean-value lemma (`A01`): If $e \ge 0$ satisfies $\Delta e \ge -b e^2$ and $\int_{D_r} e < \eta$, then $\sup_{D_{r/2}} e \le C r^{-2} \int_{D_r} e$.
  3. The proof applies Patrick Massot's formalization of **Hofer's lemma** (`Mathlib.Analysis.Hofer.hofer`) to the function $\phi(z) = |du(z)| (r - |z|)$ to find an extremal point where the gradient is locally bounded.

### 4.2 Minimal Energy Gap of Non-Constant Spheres (Task `A07`)
There exists $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{sph}} > 0$ such that every non-constant $J$-holomorphic sphere $u: \mathbb{CP}^1 \to M$ satisfies:
$$E(u) \ge \varepsilon_{\mathrm{sph}}.$$
- **Proof Structure**:
  1. Cover the round sphere $\mathbb{CP}^1$ by a finite collection of coordinate disks of radius $r_0$.
  2. If $E(u) < \varepsilon_{\mathrm{sph}}$, $\varepsilon$-regularity bounds the global gradient:
     $$\sup_{\mathbb{CP}^1} |du| \le C \sqrt{E(u)}.$$
  3. Integrating the gradient along geodesics yields an image diameter bound: $\operatorname{diam} u(\mathbb{CP}^1) \le C' \sqrt{E(u)}$.
  4. For $E(u)$ sufficiently small, $\operatorname{diam} u(\mathbb{CP}^1)$ is strictly less than the injectivity radius and convexity radius of $M$.
  5. Thus, the entire image $u(\mathbb{CP}^1)$ is contained in a single contractible coordinate chart $U \subset M$.
  6. On $U$, the closed 2-form $\omega$ is exact: $\omega = d\alpha$.
  7. By Stokes' theorem on the closed manifold $\mathbb{CP}^1$:
     $$E(u) = \int_{\mathbb{CP}^1} u^* \omega = \int_{\mathbb{CP}^1} d(u^* \alpha) = 0.$$
  8. By positivity, $E(u) = 0 \implies u$ is constant, establishing the contrapositive! This proof requires zero bubbling, zero puncture removal, and no rationality assumptions on $[\omega]$.

### 4.3 Cylinder Localization & Exponential Decay (Tasks `A08`, `A09`, `A10`)
On a long conformal cylinder $[-L, L] \times S^1$, if energy is small ($E(v) < \varepsilon_{\mathrm{neck}}$), energy and diameter decay exponentially away from the ends.
- **Target Monotonicity (`A08`)**: If $x \in u(\operatorname{int} \Sigma)$ and $u(\partial \Sigma) \cap B_r(x) = \emptyset$, then $E(u; u^{-1}(B_r(x))) \ge c r^2$.
- **Cylinder Localization (`A09`)**: Bounded total energy and small loop diameter force the cylinder interior $[-L+1, L-1] \times S^1$ into a single target chart.
- **Exponential Energy Decay (`A10`)**: Let $F(T) = E(v; [-L+T, L-T] \times S^1)$. By Stokes' theorem, $F(T)$ is bounded by the loop actions at the boundaries:
  $$F(T) \le -C F'(T) \implies F(T) \le C_0 e^{-aT} E(v),$$
  $$\operatorname{diam} v([-L+T, L-T] \times S^1) \le C_0 e^{-aT/2} \sqrt{E(v)}.$$

### 4.4 Singularity Removal at Punctures (Tasks `A11`, `A12`)
Any finite-energy $J$-holomorphic map $u: D_1 \setminus \{0\} \to M$ extends smoothly to $D_1$.
- In cylindrical coordinates $(s, t) \in [0, \infty) \times S^1$ via $z = e^{-(s+it)}$, the cylinder decay implies $E(u; D_r) \le C r^{2\alpha}$ for some $\alpha > 0$.
- Gradient decay: $|du(z)| \le C |z|^{\alpha - 1}$.
- Choosing $p > 2$ with $p(1 - \alpha) < 2$, the gradient is $L^p$-integrable: $u \in W^{1, p}(D_1, M)$.
- Sobolev embedding $W^{1, p} \hookrightarrow C^{0, 1 - 2/p}$ provides continuous extension across 0.
- Distributional extension across the puncture satisfies $\bar{\partial}_J u = 0$ weakly; nonlinear elliptic bootstrap (`A05`) proves smoothness.

### 4.5 The Universal Energy Threshold $h > 0$ (Task `B01`)
We fix once and for all:
$$0 < h \le \min \left( \frac{\varepsilon_{\mathrm{reg}}}{2}, \, \varepsilon_{\mathrm{sph}}, \, \frac{\varepsilon_{\mathrm{neck}}}{2} \right).$$
Every subsequent step in the induction quantifies over this fixed record; re-choosing $h$ dynamically is strictly prohibited.

### 4.6 Calibrated Moving Centers & Radii (Task `B02`)
For a local concentration point $0 \in D_1$ with defect mass $m \ge \varepsilon_{\mathrm{reg}} \ge h$:
1. Choose center $a_n \in \overline{D_{1/2}}$ maximizing the Euclidean gradient $|df_n|$.
   - Because $m > 0$, the maximum gradient tends to $\infty$ and $a_n \to 0$.
2. By the Intermediate Value Theorem applied to the continuous radial energy function $r \mapsto E(f_n; D_r(a_n))$, choose radius $\lambda_n > 0$ satisfying the **exact calibration equation**:
   $$E(f_n; D_{\lambda_n}(a_n)) = m - \frac{h}{2}.$$
3. Since $m - h/2 > 0$ and $E(f_n; D_r(a_n)) \to m + E(f; D_r) > m - h/2$ for fixed $r > 0$, we have $\lambda_n \to 0$.
4. The rescaled sequence $v_n(z) := f_n(a_n + \lambda_n z)$ is defined on domains expanding to exhaust $\mathbb{C}$.

### 4.7 Vanishing Neck Energy and Diameter (Task `B03`)
In the annular neck $A(R\lambda_n, \delta; a_n)$:
$$\lim_{n \to \infty} E(f_n; A(\lambda_n, R\delta; a_n)) = \frac{h}{2} + E(f; D_{R\delta}) < \varepsilon_{\mathrm{neck}} \quad \text{for small } \delta.$$
Applying cylinder exponential decay (`A10`) on logarithmic coordinates with collar width $\log R$:
$$E(f_n; A(R\lambda_n, \delta; a_n)) \le C R^{-a} \varepsilon_{\mathrm{neck}},$$
$$\operatorname{diam} f_n(A(R\lambda_n, \delta; a_n)) \le C R^{-a/2} \sqrt{\varepsilon_{\mathrm{neck}}}.$$
Taking $n \to \infty$, then $\delta \to 0$, then $R \to \infty$ ensures **zero neck energy loss** and **zero neck distance**.

### 4.8 Ghost Branching: Constant Bubbles Have $\ge 2$ Children (Task `B05`)
If the rescaled limit sphere $v: \mathbb{CP}^1 \to M$ is **constant**:
1. Since total defect mass is $m > 0$, there must be at least one concentration point in $\mathbb{C}$.
2. The center $a_n$ maximized the gradient on $D_{1/2}$, so $|dv_n(0)| \ge |dv_n(z)|$. This forces $z = 0$ to be a concentration point!
3. By the calibration equation (5), the energy inside $D_1(0)$ is $m - h/2$. Thus the concentration atom at 0 has mass at most $m - h/2$.
4. Since the total defect mass is $m$, the remaining mass $m - m_0 \ge h/2 > 0$ must concentrate at least at one other point $q_2 \in \mathbb{C} \setminus \{0\}$.
5. Hence, there are at least **two** finite concentration points $q_1 = 0, q_2 \in \mathbb{C}$.
6. Together with the parent attachment point at $\infty \in \mathbb{CP}^1$, the ghost component has at least **three** special points ($0, q_2, \infty$).
7. **Conclusion**: Every constant bubble component has $\operatorname{deg}(v) \ge 3$, and is therefore **automatically topologically stable**!

### 4.9 Well-Founded Induction on Energy Rank $\lfloor m / h \rfloor$ (Tasks `B06`, `B07`)
Rather than an informal "repeat until energy is exhausted", the proof inducts on the natural number:
$$\operatorname{rank}(m) := \left\lfloor \frac{m}{h} \right\rfloor \in \mathbb{N}.$$
- If the bubble $v$ is non-constant: $E(v) \ge \varepsilon_{\mathrm{sph}} \ge h$. Thus each child mass satisfies $m_i \le m - h$.
- If the bubble $v$ is constant: by Task `B05`, there are at least two children, and each child carries mass $m_i \ge h$. Thus $m_i \le m - m_j \le m - h$.
- In both cases:
  $$\left\lfloor \frac{m_i}{h} \right\rfloor \le \left\lfloor \frac{m - h}{h} \right\rfloor = \left\lfloor \frac{m}{h} \right\rfloor - 1 < \left\lfloor \frac{m}{h} \right\rfloor.$$
- The induction on $\mathbb{N}$ strictly terminates in at most $\lfloor B / h \rfloor$ steps!

### 4.10 Global Assembly & Root Stabilization (Tasks `G01`, `G02`)
- Local affine rescalings extend to global Möbius automorphisms of $\mathbb{CP}^1$.
- Composed frame transitions telescope along tree paths, giving the global convergence witness.
- **Root Stabilization**:
  - If the original base component $u_0$ is non-constant, it is stable.
  - If $u_0$ is constant with $\operatorname{deg}(u_0) = 1$, delete the root vertex and promote its unique child to root.
  - If $u_0$ is constant with $\operatorname{deg}(u_0) = 2$, suppress the vertex and join its two child edges. At the suppressed point, the energy defect is zero; by `C02`, convergence upgrades to smooth convergence across the join.
  - An isolated constant root has energy 0, impossible for non-constant inputs by `A07`.

### 4.11 Component Count Identity (Task `G04`)
Let $T = (V, E_T)$ be the limiting stable tree. Let $P$ be the number of non-constant components and $G$ the number of ghost components ($|V| = P + G$).
1. Every non-constant component has degree $\ge 1$ (when $|V| > 1$).
2. In the unmarked setting ($k = 0$), every ghost component has degree $\ge 3$. (In the $k$-marked setting, $\operatorname{deg}_{\text{nodal}}(v) + k_v \ge 3$).
3. In any tree, the sum of degrees is $2(|V| - 1) = 2(P + G - 1)$.
4. Therefore, when $|V| > 1$:
   $$3G + 1 \cdot P \le \sum_{v \in V} \operatorname{deg}(v) = 2(P + G - 1) \iff 3G + P \le 2P + 2G - 2 \iff G \le P - 2.$$
   (For $k$-marked trees: $G \le P + k - 2$).
5. Total vertex count:
   For $|V| > 1$, $|V| = P + G \le 2P + k - 2 \le 2 \lfloor B / h \rfloor + k - 2$.
   Including the single-curve case ($|V| = 1, P = 1, G = 0$), the universal bound across all configurations is:
   $$|V| \le \max\left(1, 2 \left\lfloor \frac{B}{h} \right\rfloor + k - 2\right).$$

Define symmetrized metric $g(v, w) = \frac{1}{2}(\omega(v, Jw) + \omega(w, Jv))$ and prove it is positive definite, symmetric, and $J$-invariant. import Mathlib.Geometry.Manifold.SmoothManifoldWithCorners

open scoped Manifold ContDiff Topology
open Metric Set

namespace GromovCompactness

variable {E : Type*} [NormedAddCommGroup E] [NormedSpace ℝ E]
variable {H : Type*} [TopologicalSpace H] (I : ModelWithCorners ℝ E H)
variable {M : Type*} [TopologicalSpace M] [ChartedSpace H M] [SmoothManifoldWithCorners I M]

/-- A compact, boundaryless, finite-dimensional smooth manifold equipped with a closed
non-degenerate two-form `ω` and an `ω`-tamed smooth almost complex structure `J`. -/
class ClosedTameTarget (I : ModelWithCorners ℝ E H) (M : Type*)
    [TopologicalSpace M] [ChartedSpace H M] [SmoothManifoldWithCorners I M]
    [CompactSpace M] where
  ω : (x : M) → TangentSpace I x →L[ℝ] TangentSpace I x →L[ℝ] ℝ
  ω_skew : ∀ (x : M) (v w : TangentSpace I x), ω x v w = - ω x w v
  ω_closed : Prop -- dω = 0
  ω_nondegenerate : ∀ (x : M) (v : TangentSpace I x), (∀ w, ω x v w = 0) → v = 0
  J : (x : M) → TangentSpace I x →L[ℝ] TangentSpace I x
  J_sq : ∀ (x : M) (v : TangentSpace I x), J x (J x v) = -v
  J_smooth : ∀ (x : M), ContMDiffAt I 𝓘(ℝ, TangentSpace I x →L[ℝ] TangentSpace I x) ∞
    (fun y => J y) x
  tame : ∀ (x : M) (v : TangentSpace I x), v ≠ 0 → 0 < ω x v (J x v)

/-- Symmetrized Riemannian metric $g(v, w) = \frac{1}{2}(\omega(v, Jw) + \omega(w, Jv))$. -/
def ClosedTameTarget.metric [tgt : ClosedTameTarget I M] (x : M) (v w : TangentSpace I x) : ℝ :=
  (1 / 2) * (tgt.ω x v (tgt.J x w) + tgt.ω x w (tgt.J x v))

theorem metric_pos_def [tgt : ClosedTameTarget I M] (x : M) (v : TangentSpace I x) (hv : v ≠ 0) :
    0 < ClosedTameTarget.metric I M x v v := by sorry

theorem metric_J_invariant [tgt : ClosedTameTarget I M] (x : M) (v w : TangentSpace I x) :
    ClosedTameTarget.metric I M x (tgt.J x v) (tgt.J x w) = ClosedTameTarget.metric I M x v w := by sorry

end GromovCompactness

## 6. Mathematical Stress Cases and Formal Guardrails

To prevent classical formalization flaws, every contract is hardened against the eight stress cases detailed in Section 6 of `gromov_compactness.pdf`:

| # | Stress Case Scenario | Informal Proof Failure Mode | Lean 4 Architectural Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Non-compact Target $M = \mathbb{C}$** | Constant maps $u_n(z) = n$ have energy 0, but diverge to infinity with no convergent subsequence. | The target typeclass `ClosedTameTarget` strictly requires `[CompactSpace M]`. For non-compact extensions, a uniform compact image constraint is mandatory. |
| **2** | **Domain Collapse $u_n(z) = n z$ on $\mathbb{CP}^1 \to \mathbb{CP}^1$** | On the original domain, $u_n(z) \to \infty$ locally uniformly on $\mathbb{C}^*$. Convergence on the original domain alone completely loses the non-constant bubble! | The `CompactnessWitness` contract explicitly parameterizes convergence through moving Möbius coordinate frames $\phi_{v, n} \in \operatorname{PSL}(2, \mathbb{C})$. |
| **3** | **Constant Base with Multiple Concentration Clusters (Ghosts)** | If multiple bubbles form while the base component map collapses to a constant, the base has energy 0 and cannot be detected by energy bounds alone. | Contract `B05` proves that every rescaled constant bubble component has $\ge 2$ child nodes plus $\infty$, ensuring $\deg(v) \ge 3$ (automatic stability). Contract `G02` stabilizes the root. |
| **4** | **Multiple Covers ($z \mapsto z^d$)** | High-degree covers $z^d$ exhibit non-injective branching and multiple sheets. | No proof step in any contract assumes injectivity, simplicity, or somewhere-injectivity. Monotonicity (`A08`) works strictly with image points $x \in u(\operatorname{int}\Sigma)$. |
| **5** | **Concentration on Calibration Boundary $|z| = 1$** | Energy atoms landing exactly on $\partial D_1$ could invalidate energy identities on $D_1$. | The exact margin $h/2$ in calibration equation $E(f_n; D_{\lambda_n}(a_n)) = m - h/2$ ensures that even if atoms exist on $\partial D_1$, the atom at $0$ has mass $\le m - h/2$, preserving ghost branching. |
| **6** | **Strict vs. Non-Strict Inequalities at Energy Thresholds** | Mismatched bounds between $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{reg}}, \varepsilon_{\mathrm{sph}}, \varepsilon_{\mathrm{neck}}$ lead to circular definitions and off-by-one errors. | Universal record `TargetThresholds` (`B01`) sets $0 < h \le \min(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{reg}}/2, \varepsilon_{\mathrm{sph}}, \varepsilon_{\mathrm{neck}}/2)$ globally before any induction begins. |
| **7** | **Colliding Marked Points on Constant Spheres** | Three marked points colliding without gradient blow-up still requires bubble insertion to maintain marked stability. | Handled cleanly in extension contract `E03` by ratio-of-differences normalization and tree stabilization. |
| **8** | **Varying Homology Classes** | Sequences $u_n$ with bounded energy can represent different homology classes in non-simply connected targets. | Fixed homology is not assumed for subsequence extraction; contract `E01` explicitly proves $[u_{\sigma(n)}] = \sum [u_v]$ in $H_2(M; \mathbb{Z})$ via 2-chain neck pinching. |
